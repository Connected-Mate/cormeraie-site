About

Alexandre Cormeraie

Head of AI & Innovation at TGV Europe (SNCF Voyageurs) · Applied AI builder · Paris

Alexandre Cormeraie is Head of AI & Innovation for TGV Europe, in the TGV Marketing division of SNCF Voyageurs, in Paris. He puts generative AI into production — real tools for real users — and built the SNCF group's first “AI Native” innovation department. On the side he ships AI apps with Connected Mate, publishes open-source tools for coding agents, and speaks at CEDEP and INSEAD. He won the Spark Spin Off prize at the France Corporate Innovation Awards 2026.

Applied AI at SNCF

Since July 2025 his mission has been to grow digital innovation in the TGV Marketing division and make generative AI a concrete lever for business teams. In a few months the AI team he leads has used, all members together, 240 billion tokens and built more than 20 internal apps, with up to 5 AI agents per task.

  • Citizen Creators programme, with Figma

    The SNCF group's first vibe-coding programme: internal apps built entirely by AI and rolled out to business teams.

  • SNCF's first MCP server

    The group's very first MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, connected to SNCF Open Data, and support for the SNCF Connect MCP app in ChatGPT.

  • Booking a TGV ticket by voice

    The first TGV ticket booked by voice in December 2023 with OpenAI, then the use case in production, and a first speech-to-speech AI built to support the phone line.

  • Patrick Code

    A self-hosted coding-agent orchestrator (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode) plugged into the group's AI providers (AWS Bedrock, Azure AI Foundry), used by several group entities.

  • Creative Studio & Presentation Studio

    Internal generative platforms, entirely vibe-coded, used by 400 to 500 people every month.

  • Apple Vision Pro and TGV INOUI Experience ↗ (new tab)

    15 headsets and 5 apps (sales teams, maintenance centres, executive committee, immersive podcast, public App Store): TGV INOUI became the first rail company in the world to offer an immersive trip aboard its newest trains.

  • An agent-ready TGV INOUI design system

    A design system AI agents can drive, and partnerships with Apple, Google Cloud and Meta.

AI app developer

With Connected Mate he designs and ships, alone and with AI, apps for iPhone and Mac and tools for coding with agents.

  • NoteTaker AI ↗ (new tab)

    Meeting summaries 100% on the iPhone with Apple Foundation Models: no data leaves the device. Featured by Apple, shown on BFM Business, 1,800+ downloads.

  • Dys Keyboard ↗ (new tab)

    A keyboard designed for people who find writing hard, approved by Apple for kids.

  • Better Dictate ↗ (new tab)

    Fast, private dictation for Mac, powered by Whisper, 100% offline.

  • AnnotateKit and Agentation ↗ (new tab)

    Point at what should change instead of describing it, and the coding agent gets precise feedback. Agentation turns it into a way of working — “Don't vibe code, vibe create”.

  • Open source ↗ (new tab)

    fable-mindset, gptimage, corporate-launcher, MacPulse, TokenWatt: free tools around coding agents and local AI.
